FAQ

Do I need Spotify Premium?

Yes. The Spotify Web API requires a Premium account to control playback.

Why don't my hotkeys work?

Spotify must have an active playback session. Open the Spotify app or web player and start playing a song first, then your hotkeys will work.

Does it work during full-screen games?

Yes! The hotkeys are registered at the OS level and work even when other apps are in full-screen exclusive mode.

Is my Spotify account safe?

Yes. Authentication uses Spotify's official OAuth 2.0 with PKCE flow. Your credentials are never seen by the app — you log in directly on Spotify's website. Tokens are stored locally on your machine.

How is this different from keyboard media keys?

Media keys are fixed physical keys that not all keyboards have (especially 60% and compact layouts). This app lets you assign any key combination you want.
